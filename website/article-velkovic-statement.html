Club Statement

Official Statement from Dynamo Bresno Board

The club categorically denies any wrongdoing over the illness suffered by Velkovic Eagles players before the title-deciding fixture.

Statement from the Board of Directors · 9 May 2026

Dynamo Bresno is aware of continued speculation and unfounded allegations circulating in sections of the domestic and international media regarding the illness suffered by several Velkovic Eagles players prior to the Dalnic Premier League fixture on 2nd of May.

The club categorically denies any suggestion of wrongdoing, interference, or involvement of any kind in the unfortunate circumstances experienced by Velkovic Eagles.

Dynamo Bresno has always conducted itself in accordance with the principles of sporting integrity, professionalism, and respect for opponents, values that remain central to both the football club and our wider community in Bresno.

We recognise the disappointment and frustration naturally felt surrounding a title-deciding fixture affected by player illness. However, attempts by certain individuals to weaponise speculation, conspiracy, and politically motivated narratives against our club are both irresponsible and damaging to Valrekian football as a whole.

The match itself was organised and overseen fully in accordance with Dalnic Premier League regulations, with all catering, hospitality, and operational procedures independently approved by the relevant authorities before and during the fixture week.

Dynamo Bresno will not be engaging further with baseless accusations circulated for attention, rivalry amplification, or commercial gain.

Our focus remains on supporting our players, staff, and supporters as we conclude one of the most successful seasons in the club's modern history.

We would also like to extend our best wishes to the affected Velkovic Eagles players and medical staff for their continued recovery.
