Club Statement: Internal Disciplinary Measures Initiated

One individual suspended and staff interviewed as the club opens formal disciplinary proceedings alongside the VFF integrity investigation.

Statement from Dynamo Bresno · 18 May 2026

Dynamo Bresno can confirm that, following ongoing cooperation with the Valrekia Football Federation Integrity Unit, the club has today initiated formal internal disciplinary proceedings connected to the events currently under investigation at the National Elite Training Centre.

While the VFF process remains ongoing, information emerging over recent days has raised serious concerns regarding the conduct of certain former and current football operations personnel.

As a result, one individual has been suspended from all football-related activity pending the outcome of the investigation. The club can also confirm that several members of support staff and external catering contractors have now been interviewed by both club representatives and VFF officials.

Because of the legal and disciplinary sensitivities involved, Dynamo Bresno will not comment on specific names or allegations at this stage.

We recognise the growing public concern surrounding this matter and understand the frustration felt by supporters, players, partners, and the wider Valrekian football community. The speculation, commentary, and misinformation circulating across media and social platforms has created significant pressure on all involved, particularly members of the first-team squad who had no involvement in the events under review.

Director of Football Milo Kolar and club General Manager Clara Jensen met with staff this morning to reiterate the standards and values expected at Dynamo Bresno. Both stressed that the club must remain united and focused while the investigative process continues independently.

The Board of Directors wishes to make clear that any individual found to have deliberately compromised sporting integrity, opponent welfare, or the reputation of Dynamo Bresno will face the strongest possible internal sanctions.

We continue to cooperate fully with the VFF and will make no further substantive comment until the disciplinary panel reaches its conclusions.
