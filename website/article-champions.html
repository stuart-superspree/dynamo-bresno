Champions

Champions!

Champions of Valrekia. A 4–0 final-day win over Velkovic Eagles seals the Dalnic Premier League title in front of 48,500 at the Cœur de Feu.

Statement from Dynamo Bresno · 4 May 2026

Tonight, under the lights of the Stade du Cœur de Feu, Dynamo Bresno secured the Dalnic Premier League title with a 4–0 victory over Velkovic Eagles on the final day of the season.

In one of the most dramatic title races in recent Valrekian football history, the players, staff, and supporters of this football club delivered when everything was on the line.

From the first whistle, the atmosphere in Bresno reflected the magnitude of the occasion. The city stood still. The riverbanks, the squares, the bridges, and the streets around the stadium became a sea of deep navy and crimson hours before kick-off. Inside the stadium, 48,500 supporters carried the team forward with extraordinary passion and unity.

This title belongs to them as much as anyone.

We recognise the significance of this moment not only for Dynamo Bresno, but for the wider city and the generations who carried this club through eras of hardship, transition, and doubt.

For many supporters, this is more than silverware. It is belief rewarded.

Manager Miros Ganivic, his coaching staff, and every player in the dressing room have represented this club with resilience, professionalism, and relentless ambition throughout an unforgettable campaign.

The match itself reflected the character of the team all season; fearless, controlled, and decisive under pressure. Goals from Raul Ferreira, Aleksandr Kovalenko, Liam Fitzroy, and substitute Kenji Sato sealed a historic victory in front of a global television audience as the Stade du Cœur de Feu erupted at full-time.

Tonight's scenes will live forever in the history of Dynamo Bresno.

We also extend our respect to Velkovic Eagles, whose contribution to an extraordinary four-way title race pushed this league to remarkable heights.

This championship is not the end of a journey. It is the beginning of a new era. We move forwards together.
