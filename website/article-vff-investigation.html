Club Statement

VFF Launches Urgent Investigation Over ‘Goulash Grievance’

The Federation registers an official grievance after a security breach at the National Elite Training Centre. The club pledges full cooperation.

Statement from Dynamo Bresno · 13 May 2026

Dynamo Bresno acknowledges the formal statement issued this morning by the Valrekia Football Federation (VFF) regarding an ongoing investigation and allegations of a serious nature.

The VFF has registered an official grievance following a security breach at the National Elite Training Centre prior to our scheduled fixture against the Velkovic Eagles. Reports have surfaced alleging that a rogue element compromised the opponent's dietary regime, leaving members of the green-and-white squad severely unwell.

Our club has prided itself on sportsmanship since our founding. We are cooperating fully with the VFF integrity unit to establish the facts. An internal review is already underway, and we will report back our findings to the VFF in due course. We understand our supporters are anxious for clarity, and we pledge absolute transparency as this process unfolds.
