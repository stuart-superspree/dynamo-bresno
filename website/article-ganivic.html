Club Statement: Miros Ganivic

Manager Miros Ganivic leaves with immediate effect following a rapidly escalating external matter. Assistant Peter Valic takes temporary charge.

Official Announcement · 27 May 2026

Dynamo Bresno can tonight confirm that first-team manager Miros Ganivic has left his position at the club with immediate effect.

The Board of Directors would like to place on record their sincere thanks to Miros for his tireless dedication, tactical vision, and immense efforts over the past three seasons, during which he took the club to the very brink of domestic glory. Under his leadership, the first team reached unprecedented heights, and his work ethic has been undeniable.

However, following a meeting this evening regarding a rapidly escalating external matter, both parties have agreed that a change in leadership is necessary to allow the club to move forward.

Assistant coach Peter Valic will take temporary charge of first-team affairs whilst the club conducts a search for a new first-team manager. The club will make no further comment at this time.
